Impact (rows) by likelihood (columns). Any impact of 5 scores at least High.

The severe impact rule. A risk that could cost Orbyt its card acceptance or its FCA authorisation can never fall below High, however unlikely. That stops a rare catastrophe hiding in the Medium band.

Quantified where it matters. For card data theft, a Monte Carlo FAIR simulation gave about a 14% chance a year and an expected loss of about £2.1m, well above the £0.8m a simple calculation suggests, because rare, very expensive breaches pull the average up.

Calibrated by appetite. The appetite statement split 14 risks into 4 outside tolerance, 9 tolerated while treatment runs and 1 within appetite, so the committee stopped treating every High the same.
