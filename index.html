About

Compliance experience, now pointed at cyber risk.

I lead compliance at Comfort Lodge Services, a supported living provider with five properties across London, where I run the compliance frameworks, risk documentation, staff governance and operational systems, and handle data protection requests.

Before that I ran compliance on a records project covering about 10,000 students across more than 500 schools, and I hold an MSc in Public Health and a BSc in Microbiology. Regulated, high stakes environments are where I do my best work.

I am now deepening that experience in cybersecurity GRC. Everything on this site is work I have produced: realistic simulations at a fictional fintech, standalone case studies and hands on labs on systems I own.
